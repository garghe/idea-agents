# Request: Automatic highlight clips for social media

From: Head of Digital Sport
Date: 2026-11-20

We want to publish short highlight clips (30 to 90 seconds) of live football matches to our
social channels within a few minutes of the moment happening, instead of the 20 to 40 minutes
it takes the editorial team today.

The idea is that the system spots key moments (goals, red cards, big saves) from the live feed
and the match data, cuts a clip with our branding, and puts it in a queue for an editor to
approve with one click.

We need this live for the start of next season. Rights for social clips vary by competition,
so the system must not publish anything we are not allowed to.

Budget has not been agreed yet; we would like a view of the value first.
